.DISCUSSION...Surface high pressure will keep the area rain-free
through late next week. Valley fog is likely to develop along the
nearby Cumberland River each night, with patchy fog possible at 
the burn site around dawn each morning. The fog will scatter out 
by mid-morning as diurnal mixing occurs, giving way to mostly 
sunny skies and afternoon minimum humidity levels between 30 and 
40 percent. There will likely be an increase in high-level, 
cirrus-type clouds tomorrow afternoon. Light winds will lead to 
poor ventilation, even during peak afternoon heating and mixing.
