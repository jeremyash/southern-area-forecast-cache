.DISCUSSION...A cold front will pass through from north to south 
early on Friday. This will reinforce our relatively dry air 
through the day on Saturday, with minimum afternoon relative 
humidity likely dropping below 40 percent again on Saturday. 
However, mid to high level cloud cover on Saturday is expected to 
limit the heating, which would in turn limit the mixing height and 
the dispersion/ventilation. The current forecast for Sunday also has 
limited dispersion/ventilation, but it should be noted that there 
has been a significant amount of variation in computer model 
forecasts for Sunday, implying low confidence in the forecast.
