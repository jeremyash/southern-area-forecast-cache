.DISCUSSION...Eastern Kentucky remains on the southwestern 
periphery of a large upper low over the Northeast United States 
through midweek. During this time, upper level impulses will 
continue to move south across our area, bringing periods of low
clouds for much of this part of the state as low-level flow will 
favor moisture from the Great Lakes working into the area. Dry 
weather will last through the workweek as surface high pressure 
settles in, with daily minimum relative humidity dropping with 
time while temperatures trend warmer. The driest days right now 
look to be Tuesday and Wednesday, with afternoon RH values 
dropping toward the 30 percent range.
