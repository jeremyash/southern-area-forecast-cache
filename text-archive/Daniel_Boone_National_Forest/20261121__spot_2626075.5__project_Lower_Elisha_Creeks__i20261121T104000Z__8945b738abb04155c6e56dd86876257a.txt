.DISCUSSION...Rain free weather and high temperatures 10 or 
more degrees above normal can be expected for the burn location
through Sunday as high pressure remains in control of the 
weather. Relative humidity will also drop to critical levels this 
afternoon, bottoming out under 25 percent. Despite partial sun 
promoting mixing, dispersion/ventilation are expected to remain 
limited due to light transport winds. The next mentionable chance 
of rain will be Sunday night and early Monday as a cold front 
crosses the area. Any rainfall amounts should be light, though, 
mainly under 0.1 inch. Cooler temperatures and more moderate 
humidity levels will set in behind the front.
