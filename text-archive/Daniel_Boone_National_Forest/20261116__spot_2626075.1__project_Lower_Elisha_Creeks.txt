.DISCUSSION...High pressure will reside over the spot area Wednesday
and Thursday, resulting in light and variable winds. The transport
(southeasterly) winds at the top of the mixed layer near peak heating
could mix down to at least the ridgetops, but these would be 
intermittent gusts at best, and would be light. The combination of 
stable conditions, light winds, and relatively low mixing heights
will mean poor dispersion both days despite sunny and dry conditions.
