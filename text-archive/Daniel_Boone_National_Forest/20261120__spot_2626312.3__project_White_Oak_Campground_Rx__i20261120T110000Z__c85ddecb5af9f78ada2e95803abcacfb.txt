.DISCUSSION...Rain free weather and high temperatures 10 or 
more degrees above normal can be expected through early Sunday as
high pressure remains in control of the weather. Humidity is 
expected to bottom out near or below 25 percent in many places 
each afternoon through Saturday. Despite daily sun promoting 
mixing, dispersion/ventilation are expected to remain limited due 
to light transport winds. The next chance of rain will be as the 
weekend draws to a close when a cold front and an upper level wave 
are expected to cross the region. Cooler temperatures and a 
moderation in humidity will set in behind the front.
