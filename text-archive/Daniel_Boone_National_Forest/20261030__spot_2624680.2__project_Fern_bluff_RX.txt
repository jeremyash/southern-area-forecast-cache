.DISCUSSION...Rain-free weather with mild afternoons and cool nights 
is forecast to continue through Saturday. A cold front will move 
south through the area today and tonight, but will be losing 
momentum along the way. It will not have any significant moisture
to work, but it will bring a reinforcement of relatively dry 
in its wake for all areas except near the Tennessee border, where 
the new air mass will struggle to arrive. An upper level weather 
system will arrive from the west on Sunday, with an increase in 
moisture and our next potential for rain. Periodic rain chances
persist into the new week.
