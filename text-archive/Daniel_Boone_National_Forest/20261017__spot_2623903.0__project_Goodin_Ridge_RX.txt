.DISCUSSION...An exiting front will take the last of the light
rain and drizzle along with it this morning. Drier air will then
be ushered into the region this afternoon as high pressure builds
into the Ohio Valley region. This benign pattern will remain in 
place through the weekend, and result in a high potential for the 
first frost of the season for many places tonight into Sunday 
morning. Several valley locations could also experience a frost 
on Sunday night. Mainly rain free weather is expected from the 
end of the weekend and well into the new week. Burning conditions
should be optimized for Sunday and Monday given these weather 
conditions.
