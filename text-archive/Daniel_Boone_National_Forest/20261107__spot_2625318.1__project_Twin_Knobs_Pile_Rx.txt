.DISCUSSION...Continued cold advection persisting through the daytime
hours today will allow for sufficient mixing to support fair atmospheric 
dispersion with sunny skies, with perhaps isolated to scattered shallow 
cumulus development near peak heating this afternoon. Surface high pressure 
moves overhead tonight into Sunday and cold advection ends, resulting in 
poorer overall lower atmospheric mixing with a more stable lower 
atmosphere despite sunny skies. This will mean poor atmospheric 
dispersion in the forecast for Sunday.
