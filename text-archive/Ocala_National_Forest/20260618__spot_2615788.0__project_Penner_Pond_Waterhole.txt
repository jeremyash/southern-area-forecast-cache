.DISCUSSION...

West-southwesterly surface and transport winds will slowly increase
after midnight through the early morning hours on Friday, which

on Friday, which will create high daytime dispersion values
at the burn site. Scattered showers and thunderstorms are
expected to develop on Friday afternoon and evening, with
isolated activity potentially continuing through the overnight
hours. West-southwesterly surface and transport speeds will 
gradually diminish on Friday night, but abundant cloud cover
will likely preclude significant fog formation during the 
predawn and early morning hours on Saturday. Numerous mainly
afternoon and evening showers and thunderstorms are forecast on
Saturday. Westerly surface and transport winds will strengthen
slightly by the late morning and early afternoon hours on 
Saturday, creating good daytime dispersion values before cloud
cover and rainfall chances increase later in the afternoon.
