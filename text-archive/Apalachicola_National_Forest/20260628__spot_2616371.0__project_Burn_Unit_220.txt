.DISCUSSION...
Hot temperatures forecast for Monday as apparent temperatures 
could be near advisory levels with heat indices of 108-110 F 
forecast. Light winds forecast through the morning with direction 
mostly being west/northwest but they become variable by the 
afternoon as a potential weak seabreeze and any storm outflow 
boundaries develop. Rain chances are around 20 to 40% with most 
activity developing after 1400. Fair dispersions around 40 to 50 
with humidities expected to drop to around 45%. 

While shower storm activity remains mostly isolated/scattered on
Monday, there is some potential for greater coverage than 
forecast moving in during the evening hours from the northeast. 
If a more organized area of storms develop, strong northeasterly
thunderstorm gusts could affect the burn site in the evening 
hours and an isolated severe storm can't be ruled out at this
time.
