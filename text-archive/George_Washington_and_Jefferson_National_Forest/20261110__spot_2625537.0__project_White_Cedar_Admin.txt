.DISCUSSION...
Today will be warm with westerly mixing winds gusting between 15
and 20 mph late morning into the afternoon. Conditions will be 
sunny with afternoon minimum RH values both today and tomorrow
between 30 and 40 percent. This could make for a brief period of
slightly enhanced fire weather conditions at the burn site which 
looks especially elevated and where the potential for fire growth 
will be possible. Tree canopies have dropped most of their leaves 
and there is plenty of fuel on the ground, making for burnable, 
cured vegetation. Mixing winds turn more northwesterly and lighten
overnight, before increasing again during the day from a WNW 
direction tomorrow.
