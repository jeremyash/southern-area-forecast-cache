Good afternoon.

Tranquil weather and mostly clear skies will prevail through the 
forecast period. Near calm to light NE 20-ft winds can be 
expected overnight. On Saturday, breezy northeast winds can be 
expected as a cold front brings in a slightly cooler airmass. Even
so, clear skies are expected to prevail. Expect NE winds around 
10 mph with gusts to near 20 mph through the day. Transport winds

3800 ft. Minimum RH values will only dip into the mid-40 percent 
range.

into Sunday, though a few high clouds are possible Sunday morning.
Winds will likely be slightly breezier at 10-12 mph with gusts 
over 20 mph possible through the day. Mixing heights Sunday are
forecast to be a bit higher, peaking around 4600 ft.
