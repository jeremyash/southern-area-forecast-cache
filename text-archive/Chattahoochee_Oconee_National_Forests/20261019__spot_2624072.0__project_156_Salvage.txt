.DISCUSSION...

Dry, sunny, and mild conditions continue over the burn site the 
next few days. On Tuesday, RH will likely bottom out around 22
percent during the afternoon. 20ft winds start out light in the 
morning, then increasing to around 5 mph out of the NE during the 
afternoon. Transport winds also out of the NE at around 7 to 10 
mph. Mixing heights peak at around 4Kft during the afternoon. 
Conditions will be pretty similar on Wednesday, but rising 
dewpoints will keep min RH values closer to 32 percent.
