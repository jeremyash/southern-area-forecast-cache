.DISCUSSION...

Good afternoon. Dry conditions are expected to persist at the burn 
site tomorrow. Relative humidity values will start the morning above 
95 percent, but quickly drop between 8 and 9 AM, then drop below 50 
percent after 11 AM. Minimum RH values are forecast to be 34 percent 
between 2-4 PM. 20 ft winds will be near calm at ignition time, 
eventually becoming W at 8 mph or less during the afternoon. Transport 
will be from the W at 12-15 mph through the afternoon. Mixing heights 
will peak around 4000 ft will contribute to fair smoke dispersal in the
afternoon. Scattered to broken mid to high clouds will persist through
the day. For thursday morning, some patchy fog is possible, particularly 
in low lying locations but impacts are limited at this time.
