Another mostly clear and dry day expected for Tuesday into 
Tuesday night. 20 ft winds will pick up during the morning from NE
at 5-10 mph, generally closer to 5 mph. Afternoon RH values will 
fall into the upper 20s. Mixing heights will rise between 3.5-4

between 95-100%. Right now LVORI values over the burn site are 
forecast to be 6, but there are very nearby values approaching 7 
and 8. This time will need to be monitored for smoke fog.
