.DISCUSSION...

Good morning. Dry conditions are expected to persist at the burn 
site today and tomorrow. Relative humidity values will start the 
morning above 95 percent, but quickly drop between 8 and 9 AM, then 
drop below 50 percent after 11 AM. Minimum RH values are forecast to 
be 34 percent at 3-4 PM. 20 ft winds will be nearly calm at ignition 
time, eventually becoming W at 3 mph or less during the afternoon. 
Transport winds are forecast to be NE at about 5 mph from 8-9 AM, 
then shift to NW by mid-morning. Transport winds will them be W/NW 
at 6-8 mph through the afternoon. Mixing heights peaking just above 
3000 ft will contribute to fair smoke dispersal in the afternoon. 
Scattered coverage of upper level clouds will become broken in the 
afternoon, clearing out during the evening.

While skies will be mostly clears and winds will be light overnight 
into Wednesday morning, RH values are currently forecast to peak at 
87 percent. Thus, the chances of fog development will be low over 
the burn site. Conditions will be similar during the daytime 
Wednesday, though both 20 ft winds and transport winds will be a few 
mph greater in the afternoon compared to today. These winds, 
combined with lesser cloud cover and higher mixing heights (around 
3500-4000 ft) will allow for good smoke dispersal on Wednesday 
afternoon. Please feel free to contact us if you have any further 
questions or concerns.
