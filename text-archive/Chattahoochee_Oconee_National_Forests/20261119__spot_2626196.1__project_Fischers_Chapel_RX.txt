.DISCUSSION...

Good morning! Some early morning cloud cover will be possible 
across the site but should be transient and dissipate. This may 
happen occasionally through the afternoon, but should primarily be
sunny allowing afternoon mixing to drop RHs into the 30s 
(possibly a bit lower along the top of the ridgeline). Winds will 
pick up from the east at 5-10 mph, a little stronger along the top
of the ridgeline. Mixing heights will be around 4000 ft and 
transport winds will be NE to E at 7-13 mph. This will make for 
good smoke dispersal and ADI values rising to the 40s. Smoke fog 
concerns overnight should be low, but inversion may trap any smoke
near the surface, leading to some issues.
