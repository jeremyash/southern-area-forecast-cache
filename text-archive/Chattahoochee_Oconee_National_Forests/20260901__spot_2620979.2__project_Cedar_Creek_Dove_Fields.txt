.DISCUSSION...

Good morning, Amy. High pressure will lead to warm conditions over 
the burn site with near zero chances of rain. With RH values of 95 
percent or greater to start the morning and nearly calm surface 
winds, patches of fog may be present around the site. Any fog should 
dissipate by 10 AM, and give way to mostly sunny skies through the 
remainder of the day. A high temperature of 90 degrees is expected 
at around 4 PM today, during which time the heat index will peak 
around 102 degrees. Relative humidity values at the burn site will 
drop to less than 60 percent by noon and below 50 percent between 3-
6 PM. The minimum RH is forecast to  be 47 percent.

The influence of the high pressure over Georgia will also promote 
light winds. 20 ft winds will be nearly calm at ignition time, 
becoming NE at 2-3 mph by noon and shifting to E at 2 PM through the 
afternoon. Transport winds be variable for much of the morning, and 
will become NE at 5-7 mph by 11 AM and shift to E at 6-8 mph by 1 
PM. Mixing heights rising to over 7000 ft in the peak heating hours 
this afternoon will contribute to ADI values between 50-60 during 
the mid to late afternoon today. Winds will diminish after 5 PM this 
afternoon, becoming light and variable once again this evening and 
overnight.

RH values will be slightly lower during the morning on Wednesday 
compared to today, though sparse, patchy fog cannot be ruled out in 
the pre-dawn hours given the light winds. Please feel free to 
contact us if you have any further questions or concerns.
