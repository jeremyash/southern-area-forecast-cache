.DISCUSSION...

Good morning. A cold front is expected to pass over the burn site by 
sunrise this morning. Light showers ahead of the front have largely 
come to an end and no precipitation is expected at the burn site 
today. Relative humidity values will start the morning above 95 
percent, though breezy northwesterly winds will inhibit fog 
formation. RH values will drop to below 60 percent by noon and down 
to a minimum of 46 percent by 3 PM under mostly clear skies. 20 ft 
winds will continue to increase in the wake of the front, shifting 
to WNW (270-290 degrees) at 8-10 mph from ignition time through mid-
morning. During the late morning and afternoon, winds will be NW at 
12-14 mph with gusts of 20-24 mph. Transport winds will be WNW at 13 
mph at ignition time, similarly increasing through the morning and 
becoming NW at 20-24 mph through the afternoon. These transport 
winds combined with mixing heights between 4.5-5 kft will combine 
for good smoke dispersal in the late morning through the afternoon.

NW winds will diminish to 5-8 mph overnight, with some occasional 
gusts up to 15 mph remaining. These winds should inhibit fog 
formation early Friday morning in spite of RH values just over 90 
percent. Conditions will be drier on Friday, with minimum RH values 
dropping to a minimum of 35 percent. NW winds will increase to 8-10 
mph with gusts and transport winds of 15-18 mph during the daytime 
on Friday. Please feel free to contact us if you have any further 
questions or concerns.
