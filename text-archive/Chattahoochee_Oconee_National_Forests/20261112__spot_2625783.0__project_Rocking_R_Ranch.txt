.DISCUSSION...

Good morning, a cold front continues to move south of the burn site, 
with showers from overnight having come to an end. 20 ft winds will 
be W at 7 mph to start the morning, shifting to NW at 9-11 mph with 
gusts of 18-21 mph after 9 AM and through the afternoon. Transport 
winds will be NW through the day, starting the morning around 10 mph 
and quickly increasing to 16-21 mph by 9 AM and through the 
afternoon. RH values will drop below 70 percent after 11 AM and down 
to a minimum of 51 percent around 4 PM this afternoon. Mixing 
heights peaking above 5500 ft combined with the aforementioned 
transport winds will contribute to good smoke dispersal today. Winds 
will remain NW overnight and decrease to 5-7 mph. These winds are 
expected to inhibit fog formation at the burn site overnight.

RH values on Friday will start the morning just above 85 percent, 
dropping below 70 percent by 10 AM and down to a minimum of 51 
percent at 2-3 PM. 20 ft winds will be NW at 6-8 mph in the morning 
and 8-10 mph with gusts of 16-20 mph in the afternoon. Transport 
winds will be NNW (330-350 degrees) at 13 mph at 10 AM, becoming 
more solidly NW at 14-16 mph during the afternoon. Mixing heights 
will once again climb to just above 5500 ft on Friday afternoon, 
once again aiding in good smoke dispersal and ADI between 55-75 
through the afternoon. Please feel free to contact us if you have 
any further questions or concerns.
