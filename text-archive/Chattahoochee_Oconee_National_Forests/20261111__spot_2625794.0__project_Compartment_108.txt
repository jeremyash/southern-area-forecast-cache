.DISCUSSION...

Good afternoon. Dry conditions are expected to persist at the burn 
site tomorrow. Relative humidity values will start the morning above 
98 percent, but quickly drop between 8 and 9 AM, then drop below 50 
percent after 2 PM. Minimum RH values are forecast to be 48 percent 
between 2-5 PM. 20 ft winds will be near 7 mph at ignition time, 
eventually becoming NW at 12-15 mph in the afternoon. Transport
winds will initially be out of the west around 10-15 mph become northwest 
after 3 PM around 22mph behind a passing dry cold front. Mixing heights 
conditions will start off poor before improving through 
the day with peak heights around 5000 ft will contribute to good 
smoke dispersal in the afternoon. There will be a few high level
clouds in the morning become mostly sunny to clear by the afternoon.
