.DISCUSSION...

Dry conditions expected to continue with no precipitation
anticipated through the weekend. RH values expected to be in the
60-65% range at ignition time with RH continuing to fall into the
26% to 28% range by 3-5pm. Surface winds will be light a variable
at sunrise and should pick up out of the NE by ignition time. 
Will see wind speeds in the 4-8mph range through sunset. 

RH values begin to rebound just after sunset getting up no near
80% by midnight and to near 100% by daybreak Sat. With the burn 
sites proximity to Rock Eagle Lake, RH values expected so high, 
and morning lows in the middle 40s, We could see some patchy fog 
development around sunrise Sat especially if the winds go 
completely calm.
