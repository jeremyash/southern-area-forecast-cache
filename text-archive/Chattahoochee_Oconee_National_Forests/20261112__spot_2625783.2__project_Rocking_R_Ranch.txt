.DISCUSSION...

A second cold front will sweep into the area tonight into Friday 
morning, bringing colder and breezy conditions on Friday. 
Northwest winds will remain breezy through the day with 20 ft 
winds sustained at 6-10 mph and gusts increasing from 15-20 mph in
the morning to over 20 mph during the afternoon. Northwest 
transport winds at 13-16 mph will promote excellent smoke 
dispersal by the afternoon.

Mostly clear to partly cloudy skies can be expected, though 
occasionally a brief period of mostly cloudy skies can't be ruled 
out at times. Mixing heights will reach near 5500 ft on Friday 
afternoon. Expect minimum RH values in the mid 40 percent range. 
Temperatures will start out in the mid 30s and warm into the low 
50s by Friday afternoon.
