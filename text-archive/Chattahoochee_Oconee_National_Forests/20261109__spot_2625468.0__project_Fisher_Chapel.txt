.DISCUSSION...

Good morning, Jake. A Frost Advisory is in effect at the burn site 
until 9 AM this morning, at which point temperatures nearing 50 
should eliminate any patchy frost. High pressure over the region 
will promote dry weather and clear skies today. Winds today will be 
from N at around 5 mph through the day, with occasional gusts of 10-
13 mph from late morning through the afternoon. Transport winds will 
also be from nearly due N at 8-10 mph. Relative humidity values are 
forecast to be below 40 percent for the majority of the afternoon, 
with a minimum RH value of 38 percent between 2-4 PM. Mixing heights 
this afternoon are forecast to peak just above 2500 ft in the mid 
afternoon.

Winds will diminish to 3 mph or less overnight as the center of the 
high moves closer to north Georgia, though the wind direction will 
remain N. Winds will remain 3 mph or less during the daytime and 
shift to NW by 12 PM. Transport winds on Tuesday will be 6-8 mph 
through the day, from N during the morning and early afternoon and 
shifting to NW by 2 PM. Temperatures will be slightly warmer to 
start the morning on Tuesday, which will inhibit any frost 
development. Minimum RH values will start Tuesday morning just under 
90 percent, dropping to under 50 percent by noon and down to a 
minimum of 37 percent in the mid-afternoon. The combination of 
fairly light transport winds, some passing high clouds, and mixing 
heights peaking around 2500 ft once again will not be especially 
favorable for smoke dispersal as evidenced by ADI between 20-25 on 
Tuesday afternoon. Please feel free to contact us if you have any 
further questions or concerns.
