.DISCUSSION...

Good morning. Dry conditions are expected to continue throughout the 
day. RH values at ignition time will be about 86 percent, dropping 
quickly through the morning, down to about 50 percent by 11 AM. RH 
values will be below 40 percent for the majority of the afternoon, 
with a minimum value of 37 percent at 2-3 PM. 20 ft winds over the 
burn site will be 3 mph or less through much of the morning, 
increasing to 5-7 mph with gusts of 10-14 mph during the afternoon. 
Transport winds will be W at 6-7 mph at ignition time, increasing 
through the morning up to 12-15 mph during the afternoon. Partly 
cloudy skies will become mostly cloudy for a few hours in the mid-
afternoon, though cloud ceilings are forecast to be 15 kft or 
higher. Mixing heights will peak just over 4000 ft this afternoon, 
which will help ADI increase to over 70.

As a cold front moves closer to the burn site, winds will shift to 
WSW (230-250 degrees) this evening, initially dropping to 3-5 mph 
and increasing to 5-7 mph once again after midnight. An isolated 
shower cannot be ruled out between midnight and 6 AM, though chances 
are about 10 percent and any associated rainfall should be 
negligible. Relative humidity will also increase as moisture 
increases ahead of the front. However, the aforementioned winds 
should inhibit fog formation early Thursday morning. Please feel 
free to contact us if you have any further questions or concerns.
