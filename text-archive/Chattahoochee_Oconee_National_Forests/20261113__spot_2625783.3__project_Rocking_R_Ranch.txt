.DISCUSSION...

Winds should begin to pick up by the time the burn begins at 10am
this morning, blowing from the NW at 6-8 mph with gusts that 
could approach 18 mph at times until shortly after sunset. Mixing 
heights will rise to over 5000 kft by the late afternoon and 
transport winds will be NW at 10-18 mph. This will make for 
excellent smoke dispersal conditions. Afternoon RH values should 
lower into the 40s, and may move a touch lower, but should remain 
well outside of any critical values. While sunny conditions are 
expected, can't rule out a few passing clouds as some moisture 
wraps around the Appalachians through tonight.

Tonight, winds quickly become light and variable after sunset. 
LVORI rises to around 5 by the early morning hours Saturday, 
though very dry air in place my help limit any smoke fog concerns.
