.DISCUSSION...
A cold front will approach the area from the northwest today, bringing 
another warm and dry day. Winds will begin out of the southwest this 
morning and continue into the afternoon. The sea breeze will arrive in 
the late afternoon and turn winds more southerly. There could be a few 
wind gusts as high as 15-20 mph this afternoon. Light southwesterly 
flow will continue into the early morning hours before the front 
arrives. Behind the front, winds will turn northwesterly and eventually 
northerly after sunrise Saturday. Saturday will bring breezy conditions 
with frequent gusts into the 25-30 mph range expected. There could be 
some shallow ground fog this morning, but anything that develops will 
quickly dissipate. Fog is not a concern for Saturday morning.
