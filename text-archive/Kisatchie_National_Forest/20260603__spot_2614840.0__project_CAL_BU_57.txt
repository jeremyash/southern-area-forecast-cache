.DISCUSSION...

A tropical air mass and an area of low pressure developing in the gulf
will result in showers and thunderstorms developing from around midday
though the early evening hours. These storms will be capable of periods
of torrential rainfall and frequent lightning. Storms will gradually dissipate
through the evening and should be ended by 11 PM. Away from storms, easterly
winds between 5-10 knots will prevail through the day. 

Winds are expected to remain high enough overnight to prevent most fog formation
Thursday morning although a few spots of shallow ground fog cannot be completely 
ruled out. Probability of fog development Thursday morning is 20%.
