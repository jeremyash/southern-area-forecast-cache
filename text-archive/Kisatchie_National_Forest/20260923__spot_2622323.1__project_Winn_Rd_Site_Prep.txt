.DISCUSSION...

Slightly drier air will continue to filter south across Northern 
Louisiana in wake of a weak frontal boundary that has now cleared
the region. Post-frontal winds will continue to veer a bit more 
northeasterly today, generally averaging between 5-10 mph with 
occasional gusts near 15 mph at times. These winds combined with 
decreasing RHs could spell another round of increased fire danger 
concerns by this afternoon and through the remainder of the week, 
possibly extending into the first half of this weekend as rain 
chances remain slim to none.
