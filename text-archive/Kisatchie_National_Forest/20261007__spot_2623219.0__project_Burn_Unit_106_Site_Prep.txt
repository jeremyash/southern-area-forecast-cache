.DISCUSSION...

Morning conditions today and Thursday will start with nearly 100 
percent humidity as tropical moisture near the surface works 
inland from Tropical Storm Isaias. Throughout the day, dry air
just overhead will mix down bringing about very dry conditions
during peak heating parts of the day and RH minimums 30 to 40
percent can be expected through tomorrow. Friday will be slightly
more humid as TS Isaias begins to near the north Gulf coast, but
the min RH may still mix down to 40 percent. Much better, tropical
moisture will then move inland on Saturday with some scattered
showers possible. 

Surface winds will prevail from the northeast and will gradually 
become more elevated over the next several days in response to 
Isaias. Transfer winds off the surface will sit at an ENE to E 
vector each day. All winds will turn to the north into the weekend
as Isaias passes.
