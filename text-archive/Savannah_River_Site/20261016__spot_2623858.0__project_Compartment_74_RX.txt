.DISCUSSION...
Some thin fog-stratus is possible after 5am depending on tree canopy, 
wind exposure and nearby creeks. Currently only seeing fog-stratus 
along creeks and rivers with exposed areas lifting to stratus. Any 
fog-stratus should remain thin and clear out quickly after sunrise.  
Otherwise, winds develop out of the southwest throughout the morning,
sustained 5-10 mph with gusts up to 14-18 mph by early afternoon. 
Periodic max gusts up to 18-22 mph possible this afternoon and 
evening. Winds will remain elevated this evening before abruptly 
shifting out of the northwest around midnight as a strong front 
rolls through. Gusts to around 25mph likely overnight into Saturday
morning behind this front, steadily shifting from northwest to out 
of the north by sunrise.
