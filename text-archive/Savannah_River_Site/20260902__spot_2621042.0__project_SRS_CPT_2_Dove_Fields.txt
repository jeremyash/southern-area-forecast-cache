High pressure will remain in control today with very hot temps and
heat indices up to 105 this afternoon. Some shallow morning fog is 
possible but should be quickly lift by ignition time of 8am. Winds
will be light and variable into mid-morning before strengthening 
out of the east by late morning to 8-10mph as the inversion mixes 
out. Winds will then shift out of the southeast through the 
afternoon with gusts to 10-14 mph. RH's should bottom out around 
3-4pm at ~40-45% with deep mixing heights expected through 6pm 
before the nocturnal inversion sets up and winds quickly fall off. 
Some early morning shallow fog possible again Thursday morning.
