.DISCUSSION...High pressure remains off to the north of the burn
site again today, which will keep low-level and surface winds
out of the northeast to east through the day.  There will be a
low-level inversion to start the day, and it will begin to lift
between 9 and 10 am. Wind speeds above the inversion will be
around 25 mph, and those winds will be mixing down to the surface
after 10 am.  Wind gusts between 15-20 mph can be expected from
late morning through much of the afternoon hours.  Do expect to
see a good amount of cloud cover moving up from the south today, 
so sunshine may be limited.  Rain should remain well south of 
the burn site, and generally expecting a dry day.  MinRH readings
drop only down to around 50 percent. With the increase in clouds
through tonight, along with the expectation that winds will not
go calm overnight and into Friday morning, do not anticipate
fog formation at this time.
